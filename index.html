Remember When Sparkling Water Didn't Taste Like a Chemistry Set?

Once upon a time, San Pellegrino made Momenti—a lightly flavored sparkling water that actually tasted like the fruit on the can. Wild concept, right?

It was crisp. It was refreshing. It had real juice in it. You could drink it without your taste buds filing a complaint.

Then one day... poof. Gone. Discontinued. Replaced by overly-sweet, artificially-flavored drinks that taste like they were designed in a lab by someone who's never actually tasted fruit.

We didn't ask for this. We didn't want this. And frankly, we're not okay with this.

But here's the thing: This is the Moment(i). If we don't speak up now, those bubbles are gone forever.
